# Training model

## Zones

Power zones are the Coggan % of FTP values, which match the Intervals.icu defaults. Use the athlete's configured zones if they differ.

| Zone | % FTP | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Z1 Recovery | up to 55 | Between intervals, cooldown |
| Z2 Endurance | 56-75 | Aerobic base, the bulk of weekly time |
| Z3 Tempo | 76-90 | Sweet spot sits at the top of this zone |
| Z4 Threshold | 91-105 | Sustained FTP work |
| Z5 VO2max | 106-120 | 3-8 minute efforts |
| Z6 Anaerobic | 121-150 | Short efforts, not a focus of this plan |

Power sets the target. HR is a cross-check, using max HR rather than an assumed threshold HR:

- Zone 2 should stay near or below about 77% of max HR.
- VO2max reps should reach about 90% of max HR by the end of the set.

A ramp test can overstate FTP for riders with a strong anaerobic capacity. If threshold sets feel impossible, trust that signal and retarget.

## Weekly structure

Three rides, with the hard days spread out:

| Slot | Purpose | Why here |
|---|---|---|
| Key day | VO2max, or a ramp test | Longest gap from the weekend, so freshest |
| Threshold day | Sweet spot or threshold | Main FTP stimulus, 72 hours after the key day |
| Endurance day | Long zone 2 with light tempo lifts | Follows the threshold day; aerobic volume with low stress |

Weekly targets, as totals across all rides:

- At least 150 minutes in Z2-3.
- At least 15 minutes in Z4-5.

Warmup and recovery time below 56% FTP doesn't count toward Z2-3, so total ride time runs above 165 minutes.

## Progression

Change one variable at a time, in this order:

1. Complete all reps at the current target.
2. Add time in zone: more reps or longer reps.
3. Add intensity: 2-3% at a time.

Every fourth week is a recovery week: reduce volume and keep a small dose of intensity. Retest FTP after recovery weeks, not after hard ones.
